# Screen-power assembly models

Each populated footprint uses a project-relative STEP model. F1's model
combines the holder and removable cartridge; both are separately listed in
the purchasing BOM. M3 holes have no separate body.
Screws, mating plugs, wire bends and the enclosure are not included.

## Custom models

These eight models are generated by `model_geometry.py` with CadQuery 2.8.
They are simplified original assembly geometry under the repository's
GPL-3.0 license, not manufacturer CAD. Dimensions are nominal unless marked
maximum. Use the linked drawings and the checked footprint dimensions for
mechanical qualification; a realistic preview does not prove insertion fit.

The G6C model and footprint convert the manufacturer's bottom-view pin map
to KiCad top view. Pin 1 is coil negative, pin 8 coil positive, and 3/4 the
normally open contact. The orientation mark is on the pin 1/8 end. The OGN
footprint has only its two electrical terminal holes; no invented center
mounting hole is included. Reserve access above F1 for cartridge removal
with power disconnected. No optional protective cover is selected.

Panasonic models seat the body at the PCB surface, mark negative pin 2 and
show leads trimmed to 3 mm below the top surface. Fit checks must reserve
maximum 6.8 × 12.7 mm (C2) and 5.5 × 12.5 mm (C102/C202), including the
published +0.5 mm diameter and +1.5 mm length tolerances. Bel models use the
maximum body/lead envelope; their stock -02 leads require trimming.

R8's end coating uses the full 2.5 mm diameter as a conservative envelope.
Its separate body/coating solids and simplified lead bends do not represent
an exact manufacturer surface. Relay markings and connector latches are
likewise simplified. Models alone do not qualify plug engagement, latch
access, hand-solder access or worst-case component tolerances.

## KiCad models and attribution

Other STEP files are copied from the KiCad library. Their original headers
and available directory credits remain in `licenses/`. They use CC-BY-SA 4.0
with KiCad's library exception, reproduced in
[LIBRARY_LICENSE.txt](../LIBRARY_LICENSE.txt). Sources are the matching
[KiCad 3D libraries](https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-packages3D).

| R_Axial* | Resistor_THT.3dshapes |
| TO-220*, TO-92* | Package_TO_SOT_THT.3dshapes |

Q5 uses a generic TO-220 preview and a project footprint checked against the
selected IRLZ44NPBF lead drawing. The ceramic capacitor preview uses the
selected 7.5 mm diameter / 4.4 mm thickness / 5 mm lead-pitch envelope.
Neither generic model replaces the exact part's insertion-hole review.
Removed charge-pump, optocoupler and film/bipolar capacitor models are not
part of Revision N.

`check.py` rejects missing, disabled, unresolved or incorrectly assigned
models. Native STEP export, populated renders and independent solid parsing
provide further verification. Model-assignment checks alone do not validate
STEP geometry or final enclosure fit.
