# The Segno name and logo

Segno's software, hardware and design files are open under their licenses
(see the [README](README.md#license)). Those licenses cover the work. They
don't cover the name "Segno" as used for this looper, or the Segno logo (the
files in [`tool/brand_assets/`](tool/brand_assets)). This page says how you
can use them.

## Fine without asking

- Saying what something is: "I built a Segno from the published files", "an
  effect for Segno", "based on Segno", "compatible with Segno".
- Writing about Segno, reviewing it, teaching with it, or linking to it, with
  the logo to illustrate what you're talking about.
- Building one for yourself, changed or not, and calling it a Segno at home,
  on stage or in your videos, as long as you don't sell it.
- Forks and modified versions, as long as they say they're based on Segno and
  don't use the name or logo as their own.

## Ask first

Write to tomas@aquiles.dev before:

- Selling anything under the Segno name or with the logo: pedals, kits,
  boards, enclosures, parts or software builds.
- Putting the logo on a product.
- Naming a product, a modified version, a company or a domain "Segno", or
  something easy to confuse with it.
- Saying or implying that Tomás Sasovsky made, endorses or supports your
  product.

## What this doesn't change

The licenses still let you build, change, and even sell your own version
under your own name, as long as you follow them (for example, publishing your
source and design changes). This page only covers the name and the logo.

Companies that want to use Segno under terms other than the open licenses can
ask for a commercial license at the same address.
