# Code of conduct

Segno follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull
requests, reviews, and any other project space.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work,
and accept that the maintainer makes the final call on what the project
includes. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[Tomás Sasovsky](https://github.com/tomassasovsky), at the email address on
that GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break this code,
following the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
