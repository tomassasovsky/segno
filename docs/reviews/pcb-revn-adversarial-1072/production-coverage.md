## Conditions that remain real

- The proposed screen-board location is viable by the model, but the four floor mounts have not been integrated into enclosure fabrication CAD. Standoffs, fasteners and full installed harness routing are separate enclosure work. Do not describe the enclosure manufacturing pack as finished.
- The source must maintain the stated 4.75–5.25 V at J1. The 60 C local-air/100 C winding envelope, fuse/contact working-loss allowances and bounded screen-capacitance sensitivity are engineering assumptions. They are not guaranteed hot/aged parameters or a measured startup waveform. No concrete violated bound was found; the hot fully loaded pickup comparison is deliberately tight and must not be described as generous reserve.
- USB pair impedance is nominally modeled on ordinary two-layer fabrication. The purchased cable shield/twist construction and USB-C Rp configuration have not been established from vendor specifications. Continuity, repinning where necessary, proper short shield drains and suitable existing/donor leads remain assembly requirements. The separate power leads need the specified 20 AWG-or-larger conductors and 3 A terminations; 28 AWG touch leads are not substitutes. These harness choices can be corrected without replacing the PCB.
- The selected 40-pixel full-white assembly uses the direct AUX star harness and its external reservoir. The shared 10 A supply does not support unrestricted full white on all ten pills at the same time. The new firmware still needs separate installation; software shutdown must actively lower/release GPIO17. PCB fabrication itself neither deploys that runtime nor proves the five-second shutdown wait.
- First assembled-device checks remain normal completion work: basic supply/continuity, functional screen cutoff, actual USB operation and operation inside the stated power/thermal envelope. This review does not request a pre-PCB prototype, new owner measurements or another board order in advance.

The scope supports a conditional first bare-board release after the new model reviews close. It cannot honestly support “100% perfect,” full USB certification, fully qualified assembled production, or an assertion that uncompleted external reviews have passed.
