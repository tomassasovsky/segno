# Remaining design gates after recovery acceptance

September 9, 2026. The owner accepted all six flows in the
[recovery delivery](2026-09-09-recovery-expansion-delivery.md), including the
revised connection summary. Recorded-audio repair, physical-port repair,
appliance backup, timing-track handoff, reversible preset trials and long-take
storage UX are no longer awaiting design approval. Native implementation is
still required. This is a dated reconciliation under issue 919, not a new task
board or a claim that every row of the product audit is closed.

The owner subsequently authorized the remaining non-production work. The
[completion pass](2026-09-09-non-production-completion.md) and
[recorded walkthrough](completion-previews/walkthrough.html) now cover recording
and rendered audio, tail demonstrations, timing edges, touch lock, double-press
Solo, expanded MIDI and direct USB recording. These are implemented prototype
flows accepted by the owner after the walkthrough, with the subsequently
accepted [primary-track crown](2026-09-09-primary-crown.md). This approval concerns
the demonstrated prototype, not production or undocumented reference parity.

The owner clarified the [FX requirement](2026-09-09-fx-reference-completion.md):
the same racks, effects and effect parameters are mandatory; the sound may differ.
Original factory audio is therefore no longer a prerequisite. Missing parameter
definitions remain open and must not be replaced with guessed controls.
