What changed in Segno

Progress notes on the console, the audio engine, the firmware and the interface, written from the work that merged. Follow along with the RSS feed.

  1. 17 November 2026

    Two fixes for ivi-homescreen

    Segno's two screens turned up two bugs in ivi-homescreen, the Flutter embedder Segno runs on. One ignored a screen's pixel ratio, the other crashed the app on most shutdowns. Both fixes are now open as pull requests upstream.
